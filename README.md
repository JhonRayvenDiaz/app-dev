# My Favorite Series: *Outer Banks*

**Outer Banks** is an adventure and mystery series set in the Outer Banks of North Carolina. It follows a group of close friends known as the **Pogues** as they search for treasure and uncover secrets.

## Why I Like It
- The story is full of adventure and mystery.
- The characters have strong friendships.
- The treasure hunt keeps every episode exciting.

> My favorite thing about *Outer Banks* is how the friends stick together through every challenge.
